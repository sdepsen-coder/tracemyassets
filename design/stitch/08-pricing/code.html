Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about forensic visual protection, billing, and takedown procedures.

Can I change or cancel my plan at any time?

add

Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel whenever you like from your Account settings. If you cancel, your plan remains fully active until the end of the current billing cycle, and your protected artworks remain registered in our cryptographic timestamp ledger.

What happens if I exceed my artwork limit?

add

You can keep your existing artworks protected and monitored. To add new pieces to the automated watcher, you can either upgrade to a higher tier or archive older artworks that no longer require continuous daily surveillance.

Do Deep scan credits expire?

add

No. Deep scan credits never expire and roll over indefinitely, even if you pause or change your subscription plan. They sit safely in your forensic vault until you trigger a deep crawl.

What is the difference between Standard scan and Deep scan?

add

Standard scans monitor public search indexes for visual matches and watermark traces. Deep scans employ high-entropy neural models that penetrate deeper into marketplace sub-catalogs and detect severely cropped, color-shifted, mirror-flipped, or heavily filtered copies.

Does TraceMyAssets take any percentage from settlements or sales?

add

Never. We are a software utility that gives you the evidentiary tools and proof. We never contact platforms on our own behalf or take a cut of your takedown, licensing resolutions, or copyright settlements.
